Justin Albert · Physics and Astronomy

How precision measurements of dark energy in the Universe can overlap with biogeographical measurements of the Earth, & with computing and telecommunication

Abstract. I'll briefly discuss how physics & astronomy overlaps with math & statistics, computer science, and biology in regard to the present development of miniature hand-launched propelled airships for precision calibration, telecommunication, and Earth observation.
